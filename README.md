# HPX-ARC 1025 PH Digital Radiography Solution

The INDUSTREX HPX-ARC 1025 PH is a bendable digital radiography (DR) solution from Carestream NDT designed for industrial non-destructive testing applications.

## Bendable Digital Radiography for NDT

Industrial inspection can involve pipes, curved components, and other objects where positioning a conventional rigid detector may be difficult.

The HPX-ARC 1025 PH uses a bendable detector design that can conform around curved objects while also being used for flat inspection applications.

The solution is intended for demanding NDT environments, including applications in oil and gas, power generation, and aerospace and defense.

## Key Features

- Bendable digital radiography detector
- 4 × 10 inch (10 × 25 cm) active area
- 98 µm pixel pitch
- Designed for flat objects and pipes down to 2-inch NPS
- Digital image acquisition for NDT workflows
- Integration with INDUSTREX Digital Viewing Software
- Designed for industrial inspection environments
